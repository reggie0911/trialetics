## 1. Introduction

### What This Module Is

The **Clinical Payments Module** is a tool for managing payment activities, contracts, and accruals for clinical trial sites. It helps you track what payments are owed, what has been paid, and what is still outstanding.

### What It Helps You Do

- View and manage payment activities across study sites
- Track contract details and payment terms
- Monitor accruals and outstanding balances
- Generate payment reports and summaries
- Manage the full payment lifecycle from accrual to disbursement

### Who It Is For

- Clinical operations managers
- Finance teams
- Study coordinators
- Anyone involved in site payment management

## 2. Getting Started

### How to Access the Module

1. Log in to Trialetics.
2. Navigate to **Clinical Payments** from the main menu.
3. You will see the Clinical Payments dashboard.

### Overview of the Layout

The page is organized into these areas:

| Area | Location | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Dashboard** | Main area | Overview of payment status and activities |
| **Activities** | Tab/section | Individual payment line items |
| **Contracts** | Tab/section | Contract details and terms |
| **Accruals** | Tab/section | Accrual tracking and management |

## 3. Step-by-Step Workflows

### Workflow 1: View Payment Activities

**Goal:** See all payment activities for your study.

1. Open the **Clinical Payments** module.
2. View the list of payment activities.
3. Use filters to narrow down by site, status, or date range.
4. Click on any activity to see full details.

### Workflow 2: Track Payment Status

**Goal:** Monitor what has been paid and what is outstanding.

1. Open the **Clinical Payments** module.
2. Review the summary cards showing total accrued, paid, and outstanding amounts.
3. Drill into specific payments for details.

## 4. Feature Reference

| Feature | What It Does | When to Use It |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Payment Activities** | Lists all payment line items | When you need to review individual payments |
| **Contracts** | Shows contract details and terms | When you need to check payment terms |
| **Accruals** | Tracks accrued amounts | When you need to monitor financial obligations |
| **Reports** | Generates payment summaries | When you need financial reports |

## 5. Common Mistakes & Tips

### Tips

- Review payment summaries regularly to stay on top of outstanding balances.
- Use filters to focus on specific sites or time periods.
- Export data for reporting purposes when needed.

---

## 6. Troubleshooting

### No payment data appears

- Ensure your account has access to the Clinical Payments module.
- Contact your administrator if the module is not available.

### When to Contact Support

- Payment data is missing or incorrect.
- You cannot access the module despite having the correct role.
- Errors persist after refreshing the page.

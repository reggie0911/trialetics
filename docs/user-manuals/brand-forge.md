## 1. What is BrandForge?

BrandForge is an AI-powered clinical trial identity system built for sponsors, CROs, research sites, and study teams. It generates **compliant, professional visual identities** tailored specifically to clinical studies — not generic startup branding.

Every output is shaped by your study's therapeutic area, phase, patient population, and communication goals, ensuring the brand feels appropriate whether it's on a patient recruitment flyer, an investigator SIV deck, or an internal sponsor report.

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Clinical-context-aware | AI adapts to oncology, cardiology, rare disease, and more — each with the right tone and visual style |
| Compliance-first | Outputs avoid promotional language, misleading efficacy claims, or imagery that trivializes serious conditions |
| Audience-based branding | Separate tone and visual guidance for patients, investigators, sites, sponsors, and executives |
| Ready to deploy | Export ZIP logo packs, PDF brand guides, and individual SVG/PNG assets in minutes |

> **Note:** BrandForge uses **OpenAI GPT-4o** for structured brand strategy outputs and **Replicate image models** (Ideogram, Recraft, FLUX) for logo generation. All AI outputs should be reviewed by your study team before distribution.

## 2. Getting Started

Each clinical study gets its own **study brand project**. You can manage multiple studies from the Studies dashboard. Follow these steps to create your first brand kit.

1. **Open the Studies dashboard** — Navigate to **BrandForge → Studies**. This lists all existing study brand projects.
2. **Start a new study brand** — Click **New study brand** to launch the five-step Study Intake Wizard.
3. **Complete the wizard** — Enter your study details across five steps: Study Basics, Medical Context, Communication Goals, Brand Direction, and Review. The AI uses all of this context to generate intelligent brand outputs.
4. **Generate your brand direction** — On the Overview tab, click **Generate Brand Direction**. The AI analyzes your study profile and produces a complete visual and communication strategy.
5. **Build your brand kit** — Use the workspace tabs — Logos, Colors, Typography, Imagery, Recruitment, Templates — to refine and expand your identity system.
6. **Export and share** — On the Exports tab, download a ZIP logo pack, PDF brand guide, or create a read-only share link for your team.

> **Tip:** If you already use a Clinical Trial Management System (CTMS), use the **Link from CTMS** option in Step 1 of the wizard to auto-populate study details and avoid duplicate data entry.

> **Deleting a study brand:** On the Studies dashboard, open the menu on a project card (three dots), choose **Delete study brand**, and confirm. This permanently removes the project, related records (brief, logos, brand kit, directions kits, share links, export log), and stored files in the BrandForge asset bucket. Download any ZIP or PDF exports you need before deleting.

## 3. Study Intake Wizard

The wizard collects the clinical and creative context that drives every AI output. Take time to fill it in accurately — the more specific your inputs, the more relevant and compliant your brand will be.

### Step 1 — Study Basics

Enter the fundamental study identifiers:

- **Study name** — the public-facing or internal name for the study
- **Protocol number** — official protocol identifier (e.g. `SPONSOR-001-PHASE2`)
- **Sponsor** — sponsoring organization
- **CRO** — contract research organization (if applicable)
- **Phase** — Phase I, II, III, IV, or Observational
- **Trial type** — Interventional, Observational, Device, Diagnostic, etc.

> **Tip:** Use the **Link from CTMS** option at the top of this step to auto-fill study details from your existing trial records.

### Step 2 — Medical Context

Describe the clinical profile of the study:

- **Therapeutic area** — Oncology, Cardiology, Rare Disease, Neurology, etc.
- **Indication** — the specific disease or condition being studied
- **Patient population** — Pediatric, Adult, Elderly, Rare/Ultra-rare
- **Device or drug** — whether the investigational product is a drug, biologic, device, or diagnostic
- **Condition severity** — Mild, Moderate, Severe, Life-threatening
- **Countries/regions** — where the trial is running (influences imagery and cultural sensitivity guidance)

> **Note:** Therapeutic area and severity have the highest influence on AI tone. Oncology and rare disease studies automatically receive more empathetic, human-centered direction; cardiology receives clean, clinical, trust-focused styling.

### Step 3 — Communication Goals

Select your audiences and communication objectives:

- **Target audiences** — Patients, Investigators, Sites, CROs, Sponsor executives, IRBs/Ethics Committees
- **Communication goals** — Patient recruitment, Site engagement, Investigator alignment, Internal study branding, Executive reporting
- **Patient-facing branding** — Toggle on if any materials will be seen by patients directly

> **Warning:** Enable **Patient-facing branding** if any output (flyers, brochures, visit cards) will be shown to participants. This triggers stricter compliance guidance in all AI outputs.

### Step 4 — Brand Direction

Shape the aesthetic of your study brand:

- **Starter templates** — Pre-set palettes and directions organized by therapeutic area (Oncology, Cardiology, Rare Disease, Neurology, etc.). The fastest way to get a clinically appropriate starting point.
- **Brand directions** — Select one or more: Compassionate, Clinical, Modern, Premium, Minimal, Global, Innovative, Human-centered
- **Visual preference** — Icon-based, Text-based, Abstract symbol, Scientific motif, Human-centered motif
- **Keywords** — Optional free-text terms that further steer the AI (e.g. "hope", "precision", "community")
- **Preferred colors** — Pick up to four hex colors to anchor the palette

> **Tip:** Starter templates pre-fill brand direction and color preferences. You can always override individual settings after selecting one.

### Step 5 — Review & Submit

A summary of all entered information is displayed across four cards: Study Basics, Medical Context, Communication Goals, and Brand Direction. Navigate back to correct any field. When satisfied, click **Create study brand** (or **Save changes** if editing an existing project) to proceed to the workspace.

## 4. Brand Kit Workspace

Once a study brand project is created, the workspace gives you eight specialized tabs. Each tab focuses on one dimension of your study's identity.

> **Tip:** Use the **Overview tab first** — generating brand direction there feeds the AI context used by Colors, Typography, Imagery, Recruitment, and Templates tabs.

### Overview Tab

The Overview tab is the control center for your study brand. It displays:

- **Study metadata** — Protocol number, sponsor, therapeutic area, phase, indication, patient population
- **Workspace status** — A checklist showing which areas (Logos, Colors, Typography, etc.) have been configured, with quick-links to each tab
- **AI brand direction panel** — Displays the generated brand strategy once generated

To update protocol, sponsor, indication, or other intake fields later, use **Edit study information** on this tab (or in the header); it reopens the same brand brief wizard and, when you save, returns you to the Overview.

The most important action on this tab is **Generate Brand Direction**. This sends your entire study profile to AI and returns a structured brand strategy covering:

| Output | Description |
|---|---|
| Study mood and tone | The emotional register of the brand |
| Visual direction | Overall aesthetic philosophy |
| Suggested color palette | Hex values with usage roles (primary, secondary, accent, neutral) |
| Typography recommendations | Font pairing rationale for the therapeutic area |
| Icon and imagery style | Guidance for visual assets |
| Logo directions | Concept descriptions to guide the Logos tab |
| Tagline options | Study-specific messaging suggestions |
| Patient communication style | Plain-language guidance for patient-facing materials |
| Audience tone variants | Separate guidance for patients, sites, investigators, and internal teams |

> **Note:** Brand direction can be regenerated at any time. Each regeneration creates a new record — previous directions are not deleted.

### Logos Tab

The Logos tab is the visual generation studio. It uses Replicate AI image models to create logo concepts based on your study profile and chosen style.

**Generation panel controls:**

- **Generation style** — Predefined style themes (Abstract Symbol, Typographic, Scientific, Human-centered, etc.) that shape the image prompt
- **AI model** — Choose from multiple Replicate models:

| Group | Model | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Quick Exploration | FLUX Schnell | Fast previews, lower detail |
| Standard | Ideogram v3 Turbo | Default — rich, detailed concepts |
| Standard | Recraft v4 | High quality raster output |
| Standard | Dreamina 3.1 | Stylized concepts |
| Vector Output | Recraft v4 SVG | Native SVG output |
| Vector Output | Recraft v4 Pro SVG | Highest-quality native SVG |
| Reference-based | FLUX Kontext Pro | Upload a reference image to guide style |

- **Batch size** — Generate 1–4 concepts at once
- **Custom prompt** — Add extra instructions beyond the auto-generated prompt
- **Reference image** — Upload an image for FLUX Kontext Pro, or click **Analyze** to extract a style description used in the prompt

**Concept card actions:**

- **Favorite** — Mark a concept for the brand kit
- **Remove Background (BG)** — Post-process with background removal
- **Vectorize (SVG)** — Convert raster output to SVG
- **Upscale (HD)** — Enhance resolution for print
- **Print render** — Generate a high-fidelity print version
- **Select for brand kit** — Designate as Primary logo, Secondary logo, or Icon mark

> **Tip:** Start with Ideogram v3 Turbo for initial concepts, then use Recraft SVG models for the final logo you intend to use across materials.

### Colors Tab

Manage the official color palette for your study brand. Colors saved here are included in the ZIP export and PDF brand guide.

- **Use AI Suggestions** — Imports the color palette from your generated brand direction
- **Edit swatches** — Change the name, hex value, and usage role (primary, secondary, accent, neutral) for each color
- **Add color** — Manually add custom swatches
- **WCAG contrast check** — Automatically tests all colors against white and black backgrounds, displaying AA and AAA pass/fail badges per WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards

> **Warning:** Patient-facing materials must meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standards at minimum. Aim for AAA for body text in recruitment flyers and patient brochures.

### Typography Tab

Select and preview the font pairing for your study brand.

- **AI recommendation** — Displays the font pairing rationale from brand direction, explaining why the fonts suit your therapeutic area and audience
- **Font pairing grid** — Browse curated pairings with heading and body font previews; click to select
- **Type scale preview** — Shows H1, H2, H3, body, and small text rendered in your chosen fonts at clinical document scale
- **Save** — Saves the selected pairing to your brand kit for inclusion in exports

> **Note:** Font files are not embedded in exports — the export includes the font family names and Google Fonts import URLs. Embed fonts manually in PowerPoint and Word templates.

### Imagery Tab

The Imagery tab provides AI-generated guidance on iconography and photographic style. It produces **direction and guidelines** your design team or vendor can follow, not generated images directly.

- **Icon style guidance** — AI description of the icon visual language (line weight, style, metaphors appropriate to the therapeutic area)
- **Imagery direction** — Guidance on photography, illustration, and composition appropriate for your audience and condition
- **Custom notes** — Add team-specific notes or override guidance for your organization's standards

> **Tip:** Generate brand direction on the Overview tab first — the Imagery tab reads directly from that output. Without a generated direction, this tab will be empty.

### Recruitment Tab

The Recruitment tab generates a complete **patient recruitment creative kit** — structured creative guidelines for your study's enrollment campaign. Click **Generate Recruitment Kit** to produce:

- **Campaign palette** — Recruitment-optimized color selection, often warmer and more human than the primary brand palette
- **Headline styles** — Language format and tone for recruitment headlines targeting your specific patient population
- **Brochure tone** — Guidance for patient-facing informational materials
- **Social ad direction** — Creative direction for digital recruitment ads
- **Diversity imagery guidance** — Recommendations for inclusive representation appropriate to your indication and geography
- **CTA styles** — Call-to-action phrasing guidance that is informative and non-coercive

> **Warning:** All patient recruitment materials must be reviewed and approved by your IRB/Ethics Committee before use. The AI output provides creative direction only — it is not IRB-approved copy.

### Templates Tab

The Templates tab generates **document and presentation styling guides** for all trial operational materials. Click **Generate Themes** to produce guidance for:

- **SIV (Site Initiation Visit) deck** — Slide layout, typography, and color application for the site kick-off presentation
- **Monitoring visit styling** — Consistent visual style for IMV and COV decks
- **Newsletter styling** — Layout guidance for study update newsletters sent to sites
- **Training manual** — Document structure and visual rules for protocol training materials
- **PowerPoint theme** — Master slide color and font recommendations
- **PDF styling** — Layout rules for formal study documents
- **One-pager layout** — Summary document structure for executives and investors

> **Note:** These are style guidance documents, not finished templates. Use them as a brief for your design team or when setting up branded PowerPoint and Word master files.

### Exports Tab

The Exports tab is where you package and distribute your completed brand kit.

| Export type | Contents |
|---|---|
| **ZIP brand kit** | All logo SVGs, PNGs at 1024/512/256px, favicons (16–192px), `color-palette.json`, `fonts.json`, `brand-guidelines.txt` |
| **PDF brand guide** | Cover page, color palette swatches, typography specimen, brand direction summary, tone guide, and brand voice rules |
| **Share link** | Read-only browser link (30-day default expiry) for collaborators without a login |
| **Individual assets** | Per-concept SVG or PNG download at any size from the concept cards |

**Version history** — Every time you save the brand kit, a snapshot is automatically created. Restore any previous version from the version history panel if you need to roll back a change.

**Export history** — A log of all ZIP and PDF exports for audit and tracking purposes.

## 5. AI Features

BrandForge uses AI throughout the platform. Each AI feature is powered by **OpenAI GPT-4o** for structured text outputs and **Replicate** for image generation. All outputs are tailored to clinical trial context.

### Brand Direction Generator

The most powerful AI feature in BrandForge, triggered from the **Overview tab**. It reads your entire study profile and produces a comprehensive brand strategy using a clinical-trial-aware prompt engine.

The generator adapts its recommendations based on:

- **Therapeutic area** — Oncology uses empathetic blues and purples; cardiology uses clean clinical tones; rare disease uses community warmth
- **Condition severity** — Critical or life-threatening conditions get more restrained, serious aesthetics
- **Patient-facing flag** — Patient-facing materials trigger plain-language communication guidance and softer design language
- **Phase** — Phase I/II studies may use more exploratory language; Phase III/IV emphasize credibility and trust
- **Brand direction selections** — Your creative preferences directly steer the output

### Recruitment Creative Kit

The Recruitment Kit AI (triggered from the **Recruitment tab**) is specialized for patient-facing enrollment campaigns. It applies additional compliance filters:

- No overpromising language or implied benefit
- Diversity-aware imagery guidance based on your patient population
- Non-coercive CTA phrasing (e.g. "Learn if you may qualify" rather than "Join now")
- Condition-sensitive tone that avoids trivializing serious diagnoses

### Material Themes Builder

The Material Themes AI (triggered from the **Templates tab**) generates styling guidance for seven types of trial operational documents. It incorporates your brand direction and therapeutic area context to ensure visual consistency — from patient-facing brochures to investigator SIV decks to eTMF documents.

### BrandForge Strategist

The **BrandForge Strategist** is an AI assistant persona built specifically for clinical trial branding. It is accessible via the AI Assistant panel while you are in the BrandForge module.

**The Strategist can:**

- Answer questions about your study brand and creative decisions
- Suggest color palettes with clinical rationale
- Recommend font pairings for specific therapeutic areas or audiences
- Generate tagline options for your study
- Review text for brand compliance issues (overpromising, promotional language, patient-appropriateness)
- Explain why certain design choices fit oncology vs. cardiology vs. rare disease

**Example prompts to try:**

- "What color palette works best for this study?"
- "Generate tagline options for this study"
- "Review my brand for patient-appropriateness"
- "What visual style fits a rare disease pediatric trial?"
- "Help me create a recruitment visual strategy"

> **Note:** The BrandForge Strategist has access to your study profile and brand kit data. It tailors all suggestions to your specific study — not generic branding advice.

## 6. Exporting & Sharing

### ZIP Brand Kit

The ZIP package includes logo SVGs, PNGs at 1024/512/256px, favicon PNGs (16, 32, 48, 180, 192px), a `color-palette.json` file, a `fonts.json` file, and a `brand-guidelines.txt` file. Logos must be designated in the brand kit (Primary, Secondary, Icon mark) to be included.

### PDF Brand Guide

A formatted PDF suitable for distributing to design vendors, site coordinators, or including in eTMF study documentation. Contains a cover page, color palette swatches, typography specimen, brand direction summary, tone guide, and brand voice rules.

### Share Links

Generate a read-only link to share your brand kit with team members, vendors, or sites who don't have a platform login. Links expire after **30 days** by default and can be revoked at any time from the Exports tab.

### Individual Asset Downloads

Download individual logo concepts as SVG or PNG at any size directly from the Exports tab or the Logos tab concept cards.

> **Tip:** Before distributing the PDF brand guide externally, review the brand voice summary section. Double-check that all language meets your organization's medical-legal-regulatory (MLR) review standards.

## 7. Compliance Guidelines

BrandForge is designed to produce **compliant-by-default** outputs, but it is not a substitute for your organization's MLR review process.

### What BrandForge avoids

- Overpromising or implied efficacy ("this treatment will cure…")
- Promotional patient language that implies commercial intent
- Language that trivializes the severity of a patient's condition
- Misleading safety or benefit claims
- Unsupported comparative claims

### What always requires review before external use

- IRB/Ethics Committee approval for all patient-facing recruitment materials
- Sponsor and CRO review of all external-facing brand materials
- Regulatory affairs review if materials are used in IND/CTA submissions
- MLR (Medical, Legal, Regulatory) review for promotional-adjacent outputs

> **Note:** The **BrandForge Strategist's compliance review tool** can flag potential issues in your text before formal MLR review, but it is not a regulatory tool and does not replace qualified human review.

## 8. Tips & Best Practices

**Always generate brand direction before using other tabs**

Colors, Typography, Imagery, Recruitment, and Templates all read from your brand direction. Without it, AI suggestions will be generic. Generate it on the Overview tab first.

**Use therapeutic area starter templates for speed**

In Step 4 of the wizard, starter templates pre-fill brand direction and color preferences with clinically appropriate defaults. Customize from there.

**Generate multiple logo concepts before finalizing**

AI logo generation includes some randomness. Generate 3–6 concepts across two or three styles before selecting your final direction. Use the FLUX Schnell model for fast previews, then switch to Recraft SVG for the final output.

**Run WCAG checks on your final color palette**

The Colors tab shows AA and AAA contrast badges. For patient-facing materials, ensure body text colors pass at least AA (4.5:1 ratio against their background).

**Save your brand kit after each editing session**

Version history is created on each save. Saving frequently gives you more restore points if you need to roll back a palette or font choice.

**Use share links for vendor and site distribution**

Rather than exporting and emailing ZIP files, create a 30-day share link. Vendors and site coordinators can view the brand kit in-browser without needing a platform account.

**Keep one study per protocol number**

For clean version history and export tracking, create one BrandForge project per protocol. If a study has a name amendment, edit the existing project rather than creating a new one.

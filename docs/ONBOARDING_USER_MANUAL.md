## 1. Introduction

### What This Module Is

The **Onboarding** module is a setup wizard that guides new administrators through the initial configuration of their organization in Trialetics. It walks you through four steps: setting up your company, creating your first project, inviting team members, and reviewing your modules.

> **Trialetics:** A clinical trial management system (CTMS) that helps organizations manage clinical studies, projects, and team members.  
> **Admin:** A user role with full access to company settings, user management, and module configuration. Only admins see and use the Onboarding wizard.

### What It Helps You Do

- Add your company name and logo so your workspace feels personal and professional
- Create your first clinical trial project so you can start tracking studies right away
- Invite colleagues to join your organization and give them access to the right tools
- Review which modules (features) are available and finish setup in one place

### Who It Is For

- **Administrators only.** If you are an admin and have not completed onboarding, you will be taken to this wizard automatically when you log in.
- Non-admin users (regular "Users") do not see the Onboarding module. They are taken directly to the main dashboard.

## 2. Getting Started

### How to Access the Module

**Automatic access (first-time admins):**

1. Log in to Trialetics with an admin account.
2. If you have not completed onboarding, you will be redirected to the Onboarding page automatically.

**Manual access (returning admins):**

1. Log in to Trialetics.
2. Click your **avatar** (profile picture or icon) in the top-right corner.
3. In the dropdown menu, click either:
   - **Complete setup** — if onboarding is not yet finished
   - **Setup wizard** — to return to the wizard anytime

**Note:** If you are not an admin, you will not see these options and cannot access the Onboarding page.

### Overview of the Layout

The Onboarding page is organized into these areas:

| Area | Location | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Page header** | Top of the page | "Welcome to Trialetics" and a short description |
| **Step indicator** | Below the header | Numbered circles (1–4) showing the four steps and which one you are on |
| **Step content** | Main area | The form or information for the current step |
| **Skip all** | Bottom center | A link to skip the entire wizard and finish setup later |

### Key Areas of the Screen

- **Step indicator:** Four numbered circles (1, 2, 3, 4) with arrows between them. Completed or current steps are highlighted. You can click any step number to jump to that step.
- **Step label:** Text such as "Step 1 of 4: Company Setup" appears below the circles.
- **Step content card:** A card with a title, description, and form fields or buttons for that step.
- **Skip all:** A small link at the bottom that lets you exit the wizard without completing all steps. You can finish setup later from your profile or the Setup wizard.

## 3. Step-by-Step Workflows

### Workflow 1: Complete Company Setup (Step 1)

**Goal:** Add your company name and logo so your workspace is branded and recognizable.

**Steps:**

1. On **Step 1: Company Setup**, you will see two main fields: **Company Name** and **Company Logo**.
2. In the **Company Name** field, type your organization’s name (e.g., "Acme Clinical Research").
3. For the **Company Logo** (optional):
   - Click the dashed box or the **Upload Logo** button.
   - Choose an image file from your computer. Supported formats: **PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG**. Maximum size: **2MB**.
   - After upload, the logo appears in the box. You can click **Change Logo** to replace it or **Remove** to delete it.
4. Click **Save and Continue**.
5. If the company name is missing, you will see an error. Enter a name and try again.
6. When saved successfully, you move to Step 2.

**Example scenario:** Maria is setting up her company "Pacific Trials Inc." She types the name, uploads the company logo (a PNG file under 2MB), and clicks **Save and Continue**. The wizard advances to Step 2.

---

### Workflow 2: Create Your First Project (Step 2)

**Goal:** Create a clinical trial project so you can start organizing studies.

**Steps:**

1. On **Step 2: Create Your First Project**, fill in the form:
   - **Project Name:** A descriptive name (e.g., "Cardiac Study 2025").
   - **Project Number:** A unique identifier or protocol number (e.g., "PT-001").
   - **Trial Phase:** Choose from the dropdown (e.g., Phase I, Phase II, Phase III, Phase IV, Pilot Stage, Pivotal, Post Market, Early Feasibility Study, First In-Human).
   - **Status:** Choose Planning, Approved, or Closed.
2. Click **Create and Continue** to create the project and move to Step 3.
3. If you prefer to skip this step, click **Skip**. You will go to Step 3 without creating a project. You can add projects later from the main dashboard.

**Example scenario:** David is starting a Phase II study. He enters "Heart Health Trial" as the project name, "HH-2025-01" as the project number, selects "Phase II" for Trial Phase, and "Planning" for Status. He clicks **Create and Continue** and proceeds to Step 3.

---

### Workflow 3: Invite Your Team (Step 3)

**Goal:** Send an invitation to a colleague so they can join your organization and access the right modules.

**Steps:**

1. On **Step 3: Invite Your Team**, enter the invitee’s details:
   - **Email Address:** Required. Must be a valid email (e.g., colleague@example.com).
   - **First Name** and **Last Name:** Optional but helpful for identification.
   - **Role:** Choose **User** (standard access) or **Admin** (full access to settings and user management).
   - **Module Access:** If your organization has modules enabled, you will see checkboxes. Check the modules this person should access.
2. Click **Send Invitation**.
3. The system sends an email to the invitee. They must accept the invitation to join.
4. You can send more invitations by filling out the form again and clicking **Send Invitation**.
5. When done, click **Skip** to move to Step 4, or continue inviting and then move to Step 4 manually by clicking the step 4 circle.

**Example scenario:** Lisa wants to add her data manager. She enters the email, first and last name, selects "User" as the role, and checks the modules for "ECRF Query Tracker" and "Visit Window." She clicks **Send Invitation**. The colleague receives an email and can join after accepting.

---

### Workflow 4: Review Modules and Complete Setup (Step 4)

**Goal:** See which modules are available and finish the onboarding process.

**Steps:**

1. On **Step 4: Module Configuration**, you will see a list of **Available Modules** (if any are configured for your company).
2. Each module shows its name and how many users have access (e.g., "3 user(s)").
3. Click **Complete Setup** to finish onboarding.
4. You will see a success message and be taken to the main dashboard.
5. To manage modules (enable, disable, or assign to users), click **Manage modules in Admin panel**. This opens the Admin area where you can configure modules in more detail.

**Example scenario:** James reviews the list of modules (e.g., MRace Tracker, Visit Window, ECRF Query Tracker). He clicks **Complete Setup**, sees "Welcome to Trialetics!", and is taken to the dashboard. He bookmarks the Admin panel link to configure modules later.

---

### Workflow 5: Skip the Entire Wizard

**Goal:** Exit the wizard without completing all steps and finish setup later.

**Steps:**

1. At any step, scroll to the bottom of the page.
2. Click the **Skip all** link.
3. A message appears: "You can complete setup later from your profile."
4. You are taken to the main dashboard.
5. To return later, click your avatar and choose **Complete setup** or **Setup wizard**.

**Example scenario:** Alex is in a hurry and wants to explore the system first. She clicks **Skip all** and is taken to the dashboard. Later, she opens the Setup wizard from her profile menu and completes the steps one by one.

## 4. Feature Reference

### Step Indicator (1, 2, 3, 4)

- **What it does:** Shows the four onboarding steps and lets you jump between them.
- **When to use it:** When you want to go back to a previous step or skip ahead.
- **Example:** You completed Step 1 but want to change the company name. Click the "1" circle to return to Company Setup.

---

### Company Name

- **What it does:** Stores your organization’s display name.
- **When to use it:** Required in Step 1. Use a name that your team will recognize.
- **Example:** "Global Clinical Research LLC"

---

### Company Logo

- **What it does:** Displays your company logo in the workspace.
- **When to use it:** Optional. Use it to brand your workspace.
- **Requirements:** PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG. Maximum 2MB.
- **Example:** Upload your company’s standard logo file.

---

### Upload Logo / Change Logo / Remove

- **Upload Logo:** Opens a file picker to select an image.
- **Change Logo:** Replaces the current logo with a new one.
- **Remove:** Deletes the logo. The dashed box will show the upload icon again.

---

### Project Name and Project Number

- **What they do:** Identify your clinical trial project.
- **When to use them:** In Step 2 when creating a project. Both are required if you create a project.
- **Example:** Project Name: "Diabetes Study 2025"; Project Number: "DS-2025-01"

---

### Trial Phase

- **What it does:** Indicates the stage of the clinical trial.
- **Options:** Phase I, Phase II, Phase III, Phase IV, Pilot Stage, Pivotal, Post Market, Early Feasibility Study, First In-Human.
- **When to use it:** When creating a project in Step 2.

---

### Status (Project)

- **What it does:** Indicates the project’s current state.
- **Options:** Planning, Approved, Closed.
- **When to use it:** When creating a project in Step 2.

---

### Invite Form (Email, First Name, Last Name, Role)

- **Email:** Required. The invitee’s email address. Must be valid.
- **First Name / Last Name:** Optional. Helps identify the user.
- **Role:** User (standard) or Admin (full access).
- **When to use it:** In Step 3 when inviting team members.

---

### Module Access (Checkboxes)

- **What it does:** Assigns which modules the invited user can access.
- **When to use it:** In Step 3 when inviting a user. Check the modules they need.
- **Note:** If no modules are configured, this section does not appear.

---

### Complete Setup

- **What it does:** Marks onboarding as complete and takes you to the main dashboard.
- **When to use it:** In Step 4 when you are ready to finish.

---

### Skip (per step)

- **What it does:** Skips the current step and moves to the next one.
- **When to use it:** When you do not want to complete that step now (e.g., skip creating a project or inviting users).
- **Available on:** Step 2 and Step 3.

---

### Skip all

- **What it does:** Exits the entire wizard and takes you to the dashboard.
- **When to use it:** When you want to finish setup later.
- **Note:** You can return via your profile menu (Complete setup or Setup wizard).

---

### Manage modules in Admin panel

- **What it does:** Opens the Admin area where you can enable, disable, and assign modules.
- **When to use it:** When you want to configure modules in detail after onboarding.

## 5. Common Mistakes & Tips

### Beginner Mistakes

1. **Leaving Company Name blank** — The company name is required. You cannot proceed from Step 1 without it.
2. **Uploading a logo that is too large** — The logo must be under 2MB. Resize or compress the image if needed.
3. **Using an unsupported logo format** — Only PNG, JPG, WebP, and SVG are accepted. Convert other formats first.
4. **Entering an invalid email when inviting** — The email must be valid (e.g., name@domain.com). Typos will prevent the invitation from being sent.
5. **Expecting non-admins to see the Onboarding wizard** — Only admins see and use this module. Regular users are taken directly to the dashboard.

### Helpful Reminders

- **You can skip steps** — Steps 2 and 3 have a **Skip** button. You can add projects and invite users later.
- **You can return anytime** — Use **Setup wizard** from your profile menu to come back and complete or change steps.
- **Module Access is optional** — When inviting users, you can leave all module checkboxes unchecked. Access can be updated later in the Admin panel.
- **Skip all is reversible** — Clicking **Skip all** does not delete anything. You can complete setup later.

### Best Practices

1. **Complete Step 1 first** — Company name and logo help your team recognize the workspace.
2. **Create at least one project early** — Even a placeholder project helps you get started with the system.
3. **Invite key team members during onboarding** — It is easier to set up access while the wizard is open.
4. **Review modules in Step 4** — Use the Admin panel link to configure modules if needed before you finish.

## 6. Troubleshooting

### "Company name required" or cannot save Step 1

- **Cause:** The Company Name field is empty.
- **Fix:** Type your company name in the field and click **Save and Continue** again.

---

### Logo upload fails or shows "Invalid file"

- **Cause:** The file is too large (over 2MB) or in an unsupported format.
- **Fix:** Use PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG and ensure the file is under 2MB. Resize or compress the image if needed.

---

### "Please enter a valid email address" when inviting

- **Cause:** The email format is incorrect (e.g., missing @, invalid domain).
- **Fix:** Check for typos and ensure the email includes @ and a valid domain (e.g., user@company.com).

---

### "Failed to send invitation"

- **Cause:** A system error or duplicate invitation. The invitee may already be in the organization.
- **Fix:** Verify the email is correct. Check if the person already has an account. Try again later. If it persists, contact support.

---

### "Please fill in Project Name, Project Number, and Trial Phase"

- **Cause:** One or more required fields are empty when creating a project.
- **Fix:** Fill in Project Name, Project Number, and select a Trial Phase. Or click **Skip** to skip this step.

---

### Cannot access the Onboarding page

- **Cause:** You may not have admin rights, or onboarding may already be completed.
- **Fix:** Only admins can access Onboarding. If you are an admin and need to return, click your avatar and choose **Setup wizard**. If you are not an admin, ask an admin to change your role or complete setup for you.

---

### No modules appear in Step 3 or Step 4

- **Cause:** No modules have been configured or enabled for your company.
- **Fix:** This is normal for new organizations. Click **Manage modules in Admin panel** to configure modules. Your administrator may need to enable modules first.

---

### "Complete Setup" or "Save and Continue" does nothing

- **Cause:** The button may be disabled while saving, or a validation error may be blocking the action.
- **Fix:** Wait a few seconds. Check for error messages (often in red) above or below the form. Ensure all required fields are filled. Refresh the page and try again.

---

### When to Contact Support

- You cannot log in or access the application.
- Invitations are not received by invitees (check spam first).
- Logo upload fails repeatedly with a valid file.
- You are an admin but never see the Onboarding wizard.
- Buttons or steps do not respond as expected.

**Tip:** When contacting support, include:
- A screenshot of the error or unexpected behavior
- The exact error message (if any)
- What step you were on
- What you were trying to do when the issue occurred
